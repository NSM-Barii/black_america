## 1. Where We Came From

**Before slavery, Africa was not primitive.**

- The Mali Empire produced **Mansa Musa** — by most estimates one of the wealthiest individuals in recorded human history. His 1324 pilgrimage to Mecca flooded Egypt with so much gold that it crashed their economy for **12 years**.
- **Timbuktu** was a global intellectual capital. Sankore University had **25,000 students** at a time when Oxford was just getting started.
- The Songhai Empire at its peak was **larger than Western Europe**.

**Why it matters:** The people who were enslaved were not savages pulled from the jungle. They were taken from civilizations. That was deliberately erased so the theft could be justified.

## 2. The Slave Trade

- **12.5 million Africans** were loaded onto slave ships
- **1.8 million died** on the Middle Passage alone and were thrown into the Atlantic
- It ran from roughly **1500 to 1900** — 400 years
- **9 European nations** participated — Portugal started it, Britain became the largest trader
- Portugal and Britain alone moved **70%** of all Africans transported
- It was **chattel slavery** — legally classified as property, status passed to your children at birth. That is what made it different from every other form of slavery before it.

---

## 3. The Breaking Process

Slavery didn't just physically oppress Black people. It ran a deliberate program to destroy identity:

- **Name taken** — replaced with the slave owner's name. This is why Malcolm X took the X — it represented the African family name that was stolen.
- **Language destroyed** — people from different ethnic groups were deliberately mixed so they couldn't communicate
- **Religion banned** — African spiritual practices outlawed. Drums banned because they were communication.
- **Family separated** — at the auction block, deliberately, as policy. Solidarity creates resistance. So they destroyed it.

**Why it matters:** The damage didn't end when slavery ended. 400 years of being told you are subhuman leaves marks that don't disappear with a law.

## 4. Haiti — The Most Important Story in Black History

- Haiti (then Saint-Domingue) was the **most profitable colony in the world** — 40% of Europe's sugar, 50% of its coffee
- **500,000 enslaved Africans**, conditions so brutal the average person died within **7 years** of arrival
- In **1791** the revolution began — ignited by **Dutty Boukman** at a Vodou ceremony in the forest
- **Toussaint L'Ouverture** led the military campaign — defeated the French, the British (60,000 soldiers — their largest expedition ever), and the Spanish **simultaneously**
- Napoleon betrayed Toussaint, arrested him, sent him to a freezing prison in the French Alps where he died in **1803**
- **Jean-Jacques Dessalines** finished it — Haiti declared independence **January 1, 1804**
- **First Black republic in history. First nation founded by formerly enslaved people.**
- Napoleon's defeat in Haiti caused him to sell the Louisiana Territory to the US — **Haiti literally shaped the geography of America**
- The world's response: France demanded **150 million francs** — Haiti had to pay France for its own freedom. Haiti didn't finish paying until **1947**. That debt is why Haiti is the poorest country in the Western Hemisphere. Not because Haitians are incapable. Because France spent 122 years extracting their wealth as punishment for winning.

## 5. Black Wall Street — 1921

- Greenwood District, Tulsa Oklahoma — the most prosperous Black community in American history
- **300+ Black owned businesses** — hotels, hospitals, law offices, banks, newspapers
- A fully self-sufficient Black economy
- On **May 31 – June 1, 1921** a white mob — with the support of local government and Tulsa police — attacked Greenwood
- The city **deputized members of the mob**. Private planes shot at and dropped incendiary devices on the community from the air.
- In **18 hours**: 35 square blocks burned, 1,256 homes destroyed, **300 Black people killed**, 10,000 left homeless, $200 million in property destroyed in today's dollars
- **No one was ever charged.** Insurance claims denied. The city made it harder to rebuild. It was erased from Oklahoma history books for decades.

## 6. Reconstruction — The First Betrayal

- After the Civil War Black Americans used new political rights to build real power — Black senators, congressmen, sheriffs, judges, schools, land ownership
- It was **working**
- In **1877** the federal government made a political deal — the Compromise of 1877 — and withdrew federal troops from the South in exchange for a presidency
- Without protection white supremacist militias — KKK, White League, Red Shirts — violently overthrew every Black led government in the South. **Legally. Systematically.**
- What followed: Jim Crow, convict leasing, 4,000+ lynchings, complete disenfranchisement
- **The lesson:** The system built the shield then removed it the moment it became politically inconvenient.

## 7. Slavery Never Actually Ended

Read the 13th Amendment:
> *"Neither slavery nor involuntary servitude, **except as a punishment for crime**... shall exist within the United States."*

That exception was immediately exploited:

- **Black Codes** criminalized Black existence — vagrancy, unemployment, anything
- **Convict leasing** — arrested, convicted in sham trials, leased to coal mines, railroads, lumber camps as forced labor for nothing. Death rates in some camps: **30-40% per year**. Companies had no incentive to keep them alive — they could just request more.
- Who profited: **US Steel, Tennessee Coal, the entire Southern railroad network**
- It wasn't abolished until **1941** — and only because the government needed Black men for World War II, not out of morality
- The same constitutional exception enables **prison labor today**

## 8. The War on Drugs — They Admitted It

Nixon's domestic policy chief **John Ehrlichman** admitted in a 1994 interview (published 2016):

> *"We knew we couldn't make it illegal to be against the war or Black, but by getting the public to associate the hippies with marijuana and Blacks with heroin, and then criminalizing both heavily, we could disrupt those communities. Did we know we were lying about the drugs? Of course we did."*

- **Crack vs powder cocaine**: same drug, two forms. 5 grams of crack = 5-year mandatory minimum. 500 grams of powder = same sentence. **100:1 ratio**. Crack was cheap — Black communities. Powder was expensive — white communities.
- Black and white Americans use drugs at **roughly equal rates**. Black Americans are **3.73x more likely** to be arrested for marijuana.
- US prison population went from **200,000 in 1971** to **2.3 million by 2008**. The largest prison population in the world.

## 9. COINTELPRO — The Government Destroyed Black Leadership

The FBI under J. Edgar Hoover ran a covert program specifically designed to destroy Black political organizations:

- **Marcus Garvey** — called "the most dangerous Black man in America" by Hoover. FBI infiltrated his organization, built a legal case, had him deported. The largest Black mass movement in history dismantled.
- **Malcolm X** — under FBI surveillance from 1953. Informants embedded in every organization he built. FBI had knowledge of threats against his life and did nothing. Assassinated at **39**.
- **Fred Hampton** — 21 year old Black Panther chairman in Chicago building a multiracial coalition that genuinely threatened power. FBI had him drugged by an informant before a predawn police raid. Shot twice in the head while asleep. **21 years old.** No one charged.
- **Martin Luther King Jr.** — FBI sent him an anonymous letter telling him to commit suicide. Wiretapped his home and hotel rooms for years. Surveilled him obsessively. He was assassinated in **1968** at 40 years old while organizing economic justice campaigns.

**None of this is conspiracy theory. All of it is in declassified FBI documents available at vault.fbi.gov.**

## 10. Malcolm X — The Full Picture

- Born 1925. Father was a **Garveyite** — black nationalism was in his blood from childhood
- Father killed under suspicious circumstances. Mother institutionalized. Malcolm split up into foster care at **13**.
- Hustler, drug dealer, burglar. Arrested at **20**. Sentenced to 8-10 years.
- In prison — educated himself. Joined the Nation of Islam. Became their most powerful voice.
- His message: white America will not give Black people freedom. Integration is not liberation. Self-determination by any means necessary.
- Left the NOI in **1964** after discovering Elijah Muhammad's corruption
- Made the **Hajj to Mecca** — saw Muslims of all races as equals. Began evolving toward a broader human rights framework.
- Founded the **Organization of Afro-American Unity** — trying to bring the US before the UN for human rights violations against Black Americans
- House firebombed **February 14, 1965**
- Assassinated **February 21, 1965** at the Audubon Ballroom in Harlem. **39 years old.**
- Two of the three convictions were vacated in **2021** after evidence showed the FBI and NYPD withheld evidence for 50 years

**He was still evolving. We don't know what he would have become. They made sure of that.**

## 11. Black America Today — The Numbers That Matter

- Median Black family wealth: **$24,000**. Median white family wealth: **$188,000**. **8:1 ratio.**
- Black unemployment has been **2x white unemployment** every decade on record
- Black homeownership: **44%**. White: **73%**. Gap is larger now than when the Fair Housing Act passed in 1968.
- Black Americans are **13% of the population** but **38% of the prison population**
- Black men incarcerated at **5x the rate** of white men
- Black maternal mortality: **2.6x higher** than white women
- **None of this is random.** Every number traces directly to a specific historical policy.
