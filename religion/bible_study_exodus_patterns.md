# Bible Study — Exodus Patterns and Black America

## The Theme
**Oppression vs. Liberty**

The story of Exodus is not ancient history. It is a pattern that has repeated itself in Black American history with precise consistency. The same actors, the same sequence, the same outcome.

---

## The Patterns

| Exodus | Black America |
|---|---|
| Joseph sold into slavery by his own brothers | Africans sold to European slave traders by other Africans |
| Joseph arrived in Egypt as a slave | Africans arrived in America as slaves |
| Joseph rose to power and built something | Reconstruction — Black people built wealth, held office, owned land |
| New Pharaoh came who didn't know Joseph | Jim Crow — new political regime dismantled everything Black people built |
| Israelites enslaved because Pharaoh feared their numbers | Black people oppressed because free Black wealth and power threatened white supremacy |
| Israelites cried out and God heard them | Enslaved Africans built the hush harbors and prayed the same prayer |
| God raised up Moses as a deliverer | God raised up Frederick Douglass, Harriet Tubman, Martin Luther King Jr. |
| Pharaoh hardened his heart and kept refusing | Every broken promise — Emancipation, Reconstruction, Civil Rights — met with new forms of resistance |
| God destroyed the empire anyway | The Confederacy fell. Jim Crow fell. Every Pharaoh falls. |

---

## The Verses — Read In This Order

**1. Start here — God hears the cry**
Exodus 2:23-25
*"The Israelites groaned in their slavery and cried out, and their cry for help because of their slavery went up to God. God heard their groaning and he remembered his covenant. God looked on the Israelites and was concerned about them."*

**2. God reveals himself through liberation**
Exodus 3:7-10
*"The Lord said, I have indeed seen the misery of my people in Egypt. I have heard them crying out because of their slave drivers, and I am concerned about their suffering. So I have come down to rescue them."*

**3. God reveals his name at the moment of liberation**
Exodus 6:2-6
*"God also said to Moses, I am the Lord. I appeared to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob as God Almighty, but by my name the Lord I did not make myself fully known to them. I also established my covenant with them to give them the land of Canaan. Moreover, I have heard the groaning of the Israelites, whom the Egyptians are enslaving, and I have remembered my covenant. Therefore, say to the Israelites: I am the Lord, and I will bring you out from under the yoke of the Egyptians."*

**4. God judges the gods of the oppressor**
Exodus 12:12
*"On that same night I will pass through Egypt and strike down every firstborn of both people and animals, and I will bring judgment on all the gods of Egypt. I am the Lord."*

**5. What they gave enslaved people instead**
Ephesians 6:5
*"Slaves, obey your earthly masters with respect and fear, and with sincerity of heart, just as you would obey Christ."*

---

## Key Facts for Discussion

- The Slave Bible removed 90% of the Old Testament including the entire book of Exodus
- Each plague targeted a specific Egyptian god — God was dismantling the religious justification for Pharaoh's power
- Yahweh — God's personal name — was revealed specifically in the context of liberating enslaved people
- The same name was on the lips of slaveholders every Sunday while they used Ephesians 6:5 to command submission
- Joseph's story — sold by his own people, enslaved in a foreign land, rose to power, descendants re-enslaved — mirrors the Black American experience across centuries

---

## The Closing Question

**If the pattern has repeated this many times — who is Pharaoh in 2026, and where are we in the story?**
